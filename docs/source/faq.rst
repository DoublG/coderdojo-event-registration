Frequently asked questions
=============================

Is it really free?
    Yes — every dojo session is free, run entirely by volunteers.

Does my child need coding experience?
    No experience needed — ninjas are grouped by age and skill on the
    day, not in advance.

Do parents need to stay?
    Under-12s need an adult on site; older kids can be dropped off.

Is there a waitlist if a session is full?
    Yes — sign up anyway. If a confirmed spot opens up, whoever's been
    waiting longest for that session is automatically moved up. See
    :doc:`families/signing-up-for-a-session`.

I forgot my password — what do I do?
    Use **Forgot password** on the login page to get a reset link by
    email. See :doc:`families/managing-your-account`.

Do I need an account to apply to volunteer or start a dojo?
    No — those applications (and the background check upload, if you're
    asked for one) don't require logging in. An account is only created
    for you once you're approved. See
    :doc:`volunteering/become-a-mentor-or-volunteer` and
    :doc:`volunteering/start-a-new-dojo`.

Why does the site ask for a criminal record extract?
    Belgian law requires it for anyone in direct contact with minors —
    see :doc:`volunteering/background-check` for exactly what's needed
    and how it's handled.

Can I change the site's language?
    Yes — the language switcher in the site menu offers English,
    Nederlands and Français. The pages for families (finding a dojo,
    sessions and signing up, your account and your children, mail
    preferences) are translated; the pages for volunteers and dojo teams
    follow. The texts dojos write themselves (descriptions, updates,
    questions) are shown as the dojo wrote them.
