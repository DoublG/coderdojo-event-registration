Logging in with a link
======================

You can log in with a password, or with a **login link**: a link we mail
you each time you log in, so there's no password to remember. You choose
which, and you can switch whenever you like. It works the same for every
account: parents, volunteers, the organisation and children's own logins.

Logging in
----------

On the login page, choose **Log in with an emailed link**, enter your email
address and choose **Email me a login link**. Open the mail and click the
link; the page asks whether to log in, and you're in. A link works once,
within 15 minutes, and logs in the device you open it on.

If you use two-step login, you're still asked for your second step after
the link (see :doc:`two-step-login`).

Nothing arrived? Look in your spam folder, or ask again after a minute. If
your account logs in with a password, the mail says so, with a link to
choose a new password.

Switching to a login link
-------------------------

On your account page, open **Sign-in security** and choose **Switch to a
login link**. Confirm with your password: we mail you a confirmation, and
your password only stops working once you open it (within 24 hours). So
if our mail never reaches you, nothing changes. Once it's done, you're
logged out on your other devices.

You can also choose a login link when you create your account (see
:doc:`creating-an-account`).

Switching back to a password
----------------------------

On **Sign-in security**, choose **Use a password instead** and choose a
password. From then on you log in with it, and login links stop working.

Confirming it's you without a password
--------------------------------------

Some changes ask you to confirm it's you first: turning off two-step
login, changing your email address or deleting your account, for example.
With a password, you type it in. With a login link, having logged in during
the last ten minutes is enough; otherwise choose **Mail me a confirmation
link**. That link logs you in again and brings you back to the page.

We mail you whenever the way you log in changes. If you didn't do that
yourself, contact us straight away.
