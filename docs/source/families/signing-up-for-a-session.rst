Signing up for a session
==========================

You'll need to be logged in to your family account to sign up (see
:doc:`creating-an-account`).

#. Find the session — via a dojo's page, the **Events** list, or the
   homepage's **Upcoming sessions**.
#. Open the session and tick which of your children you'd like to sign
   up for it. You can select more than one at a time.
#. Submit. Each selected child is registered individually — a child
   already signed up for that session is skipped and flagged as such
   rather than double-booked.

Mails about your booking
------------------------

After you sign up, you (and any other parent on the account, plus a child
with their own login and email) get a confirmation mail, or a waiting-list
mail if the session was full. Two days before the session you get a
reminder; you can switch reminders off on your **Mail preferences** page
(see :doc:`mail-preferences`).

If a session shows **Registrations closed** instead of a sign-up option,
the dojo has stopped taking new sign-ups for it (this normally happens
once attendance for that session has already been checked) — get in
touch with the dojo team directly if you still need a spot.

Featured events
----------------

Some events are **featured**: CoderDojo Belgium highlights them for a while
at the top of the homepage, first in **Upcoming sessions**, above the
**Events** list or above the dojo finder's results. A featured event is
signed up for like any other; the highlight disappears once the event
starts.

Events organised by CoderDojo Belgium
--------------------------------------

Besides the sessions dojos run, CoderDojo Belgium organises a few events
itself, such as CoderDojo Girlz and Coolest Projects. Their page says
**Organised by CoderDojo Belgium** instead of naming a dojo.

Some of these events take registrations on their own website. Their page
then has a **Register on …** button that opens that website, instead of
the sign-up form, and the **Events** list shows the same label. You
register there, not on your family account, so the event doesn't appear
on your account page and you get no booking mails from us about it.

Girls' sessions
----------------

Some sessions are labelled **Girls' session**. They're aimed at girls, but
the label never stops anyone from signing up.

Waiting lists
--------------

Sessions have a limited number of places. If a session is full when you
sign up, your child is placed on the **waiting list** instead of being
turned away outright — you'll see this clearly on the confirmation. If a
confirmed spot later opens up (someone cancels), the longest-waiting child
for that session is automatically promoted to confirmed, and we mail you
when that happens. No action is needed on your part.

Cancelling
-----------

If your plans change, cancel from your family account page (see
:doc:`managing-your-account`) rather than just not turning up — that's
also what frees the spot for whoever's next on the waiting list.
