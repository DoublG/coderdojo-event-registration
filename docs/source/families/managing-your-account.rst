Managing your account
=======================

Logging in
-----------

Use the **Log in** link and enter your email and password. (Your
username also works if you know it, but email is what's asked for.)

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password** on the login page — you'll
get an email with a link to set a new one.

Your family page
------------------

Once logged in, your family page lists each of your children and their
upcoming sessions (including anything they're on the waiting list for,
clearly marked). From here you can:

- **Add a child** — name, date of birth, an optional icon, experience
  level, and any allergies or notes for mentors.
- **Edit a child's details** — click their name/header to edit it inline.
- **Cancel a registration** — frees the spot for the next child on that
  session's waiting list.
- **View a child's awards** — badges and milestone awards (e.g. attendance
  wristbands) they've earned or are working towards, and their session
  history.

Changing your password
------------------------

Go to **Change password** (also linked from your account page). You'll
need your current password to set a new one.

.. note::

   If your account was set up for you by CoderDojo Belgium (e.g. as a dojo
   owner or helper) rather than self-registered, you'll be asked to set a
   new password the first time you log in with the temporary one you were
   emailed — you can't skip past this step.
