Two-step login
==============

With two-step login, logging in takes your password **and** a second step.
Someone who finds out your password still can't get into your account. It
works for every account: parents, mentors, champions and the organisation.
Children's own logins never use it.

Open your account page and choose **Sign-in security**.

Your second step
----------------

You can use one of these, or both:

- **An authenticator app** on your phone, such as Google Authenticator,
  Microsoft Authenticator or your password manager. It shows a new
  six-digit code every 30 seconds. Choose **Set up an app**, scan the QR code
  with the app (or type in the key under it), and enter the code the app
  shows for CoderDojo Belgium.
- **A passkey**: your phone, computer or a security key confirms it's you
  with your fingerprint, face or PIN. There's nothing to type, and a fake
  site can't trick it. Choose **Add a passkey** and follow what your
  browser asks. You can add more than one, for example one on your phone
  and one on your laptop.

We send you a mail whenever two-step login is turned on or off, or a
sign-in method is added or removed. If you didn't do that yourself, change
your password straight away.

Backup codes
------------

When you set up your first second step, you get ten **backup codes**. Save
them now (print them or keep them in your password manager): you only see
them once. If you lose your phone or passkey, each code lets you log in
once. We mail you whenever one is used. Under **Backup codes** you see how
many you have left and can make a new set; the old codes then stop working.

Logging in
----------

Log in with your email address and password as usual. Then the site asks
you to confirm it's you: enter the code from your app, or choose **Use my
passkey**. Under **Can't use this?** you can switch to your other method
or to a backup code.

Tick **Don't ask again in this browser for 30 days** on your own computer
or phone to skip the second step there for a month. On the **Sign-in
security** page you can make the browser you're using forget this again.
Changing your password makes every browser forget it.

Turning it off
--------------

**Turn off two-step login** removes your app, passkeys and backup codes;
you confirm it with your password. You can also remove one method and keep
the others.

Some roles need two-step login, for example champions, mentors or the
organisation's admins, when the organisation has decided so (see
:doc:`../organisation/sign-in-security`). If yours does, you can't turn it
off, and you can only remove a method once you've added another one. When
such a rule starts on a later day, you see a notice at the top of the
page until you've set it up.

Lost your phone and your backup codes?
--------------------------------------

Mail the organisation (the address is on the login page). Once they've
checked it's really you, they turn off your two-step login and you log in
with your password only. Then set it up again.
