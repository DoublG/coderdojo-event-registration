Awards
======

Awards are the badges ninjas can earn. Only the organisation creates them,
on the **Awards** page under **Ninjas** in the organisation dashboard. Dojo
teams can't create awards: a dojo's champion and mentors award the one-off
awards to the children at their sessions, from the attendance list.

There are two kinds:

- **One-off**: earned by doing something once, for example taking part in a
  CoderDojo Girlz session. Describe what earns it under **How to earn it**.
- **Milestone**: reached automatically once a ninja has attended a number of
  sessions, such as the attendance wristbands. Fill in **Sessions needed**.
  A milestone can also award a belt when it's reached (**Also grants
  belt**).

For each award you also fill in:

- **Name** and **Description**, with optional versions in the other
  languages. A text left empty in a language shows the English version.
- **Icon**: pick one of the **standard icons**, or upload your own image
  (PNG, JPG or WebP; a square image works best). An uploaded image wins over
  a standard icon.

The list shows how many ninjas have earned each award. Open an award to
change it. An award that no ninja has yet can be removed; one that ninjas
already have (or are working towards) stays, so it never disappears from a
child's page.
