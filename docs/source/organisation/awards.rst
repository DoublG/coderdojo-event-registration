Awards
======

Awards are the badges ninjas can earn. Only the organisation creates them,
on the **Awards** page under **Ninjas** in the organisation dashboard. Dojo
teams can't create awards: a dojo's champion and mentors award the one-off
awards to the children at their sessions, from the attendance list.

An award can be limited to specific dojos: fill in **Dojos that can award
it** to offer it only at those dojos' sessions, or leave it blank for any
dojo's team to offer it. An award for one of the organisation's own events
(**CoderDojo Girlz**, **Coolest Projects**) is usually limited to the
**CoderDojo Belgium** dojo this way, since it's not meant for a regular
dojo's own sessions.

There are two kinds:

- **One-off**: earned by doing something once, for example taking part in a
  CoderDojo Girlz session. Describe what earns it under **How to earn it**.
- **Milestone**: reached automatically once a ninja has attended a number of
  sessions, such as the attendance wristbands. Fill in **Sessions needed**.
  A milestone can also award a belt when it's reached (**Also grants
  belt**).

For each award you also fill in:

- **Name** and **Description**, with optional versions in the other
  languages. A text left empty in a language shows the English version.
- **Icon**: pick one of the **standard icons**, or upload your own image
  (JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP, at most 10 MB; a square image works best). An
  uploaded image wins over a standard icon. It's made smaller, and everything
  stored in the file besides the picture itself is removed.

The list shows how many ninjas have earned each award. Open an award to
change it. An award that no ninja has yet can be removed; one that ninjas
already have (or are working towards) stays, so it never disappears from a
child's page.

Assigning an award directly
----------------------------

Some of the organisation's own events don't have an attendance list on this
site at all — Coolest Projects, for example, takes its sign-ups on its own
website. For those, use **Assign awards** (under **Ninjas**) instead of a
dojo's attendance list: search for the ninja by name, open their page, and
pick a one-off award to give them directly. It's recorded as the
organisation's own award, not tied to any dojo or session, and only
one-off awards they don't already have are offered. This always works, no
matter which dojos (if any) the award itself is limited to.
