Sponsors
=========

The homepage shows CoderDojo Belgium's sponsors and partners under **Made
possible by**. You manage them on the **Sponsors** page, under **Public
site** in the organisation dashboard.

For each sponsor you fill in:

- **Name** and **Website**: each sponsor on the homepage links to its
  website.
- **Logo** (optional): shown instead of the name. Without a logo, the name
  is shown.
- **Order**: lowest first.
- **Shown on the homepage**: untick it to hide a sponsor without removing
  it, for example while a partnership is on hold.

Open a sponsor from the list to change it or to remove it. Without any
sponsors shown, the homepage leaves out **Made possible by**.
