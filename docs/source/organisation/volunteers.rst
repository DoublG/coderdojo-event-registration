Background checks and applications
===================================

Champions and mentors work directly with children, so before they can run
or help at a dojo they apply once, and their extract from the criminal
record (model 2, Article 596.2) is checked. Both are handled under
**Volunteers** in the organisation dashboard, by people with the
organisation's **background-check reviewer** role. Only they see these
pages; the other organisation roles don't.

Nobody decides on their own background check or application: another
reviewer does.

Background checks
------------------

**Background checks** is a work queue:

- **Awaiting review**: documents that were uploaded, oldest first. The
  number next to **Background checks** in the sidebar counts them.
- **Waiting for a document**: checks that were asked for, or rejected and
  waiting for a new document. **Send the link again** mails the upload
  link once more.
- **Expired**: these people can't open their dojos' dashboards until a new
  extract is validated. **Ask for a new one** mails them the upload link.
- **Expiring within 30 days**: for your information. They get a reminder
  by mail 30 days before their check expires, so they can ask their
  municipality for a new extract in time.

Search by name or email address to find anyone else.

Open a person to decide:

#. **Download the document** and check it. Every download is recorded.
   Don't keep a copy: delete the file once you've decided.
#. Choose **Validate** or **Reject**. You can add a note for the other
   reviewers, but never copy anything from the extract into it.

Either way the document is deleted straight away; only the decision, who
made it and when are kept, and the person gets a mail. A validated check is
valid for a year. After a rejection they can upload a new document.

The page also lists what the person applied for and the earlier decisions.
If they haven't been asked for a document yet, **Ask for a document** mails
them the upload link.

Applications
-------------

**Applications** lists the people who applied to become a mentor or to
start a dojo, pending ones first; choose another status or kind to see the
others. The number in the sidebar counts the pending ones.

Open an application to see what they wrote and where their background check
stands. From there:

- **Ask for a document** if their background check hasn't been asked for
  yet (or **Send the link again**).
- **Approve** once their background check is valid. A mentor who named a
  dojo gets a request to join its team, which the dojo's team accepts; a
  champion can create their dojo.
- **Reject** if they won't be taken on.

The person gets a mail with the outcome either way.

Who can review
---------------

The reviewer role is given in the site's technical admin, like the other
organisation roles, and can be combined with them. Reviewers read
criminal-record extracts, so only give it to the people who need it.
