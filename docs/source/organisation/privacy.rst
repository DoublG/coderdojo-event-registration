Privacy requests
================

Families download their own data and delete their own account from their
family page (see :doc:`../families/managing-your-account`). When someone asks
by mail or post instead, you handle it on the **Privacy** page, under
**Accounts** in the organisation dashboard.

Finding the account
-------------------

Search by name, email address or username; a child's name finds the
child's parents too. Each account in the list shows whether it's an adult or
a child's own login, and how many children it's a guardian of.

A copy of someone's data
------------------------

**Download data** gives you the same file the family gets with **Download
my data**: everything the site keeps about the account and the children
it's a guardian of. Send it only to the account's own email address.
Every download is recorded.

Deleting an account
-------------------

**Delete…** first shows what will happen: whether the account is erased or,
for a champion or mentor, cleaned (their name stays on past sessions and on
the belts and badges they awarded), which children are erased with it, and
which stay with their other guardian. Type the account's username to
confirm. It happens right away and can't be undone, so download the data
first if they asked for a copy too.

Some accounts can't be deleted here: the champion of an active dojo (the
role has to move to one of the dojo's mentors first), an account with an
organisation role (take the role away first) and a child's own login (it's
removed by their parent, or goes with the family's account).

Changing someone's email address
--------------------------------

Families change their own address from their family page. **Change email…**
is for a family that can't get into its old mailbox any more (and so can't
confirm anything from it, or reset its password). Check who you're dealing
with by other means first, such as a phone call or their children's
details: whoever has the new mailbox gets the account. Enter the new
address and tick that you checked.

We mail a link to the new address, valid for 24 hours. The address only
changes once they open it and confirm, and they don't need to log in for
that. Then the old address is told, every session on the account ends, and
the page offers them a link to set a new password. Accounts with an
organisation role, technical administrators and a child's own login can't
be changed here.

Needs attention
---------------

Accounts that haven't logged in for two years are deleted automatically.
A champion of an active dojo is held back until the role moves: they're
listed under **Needs attention**, with the date their account is due to be
cleaned. The dojo's mentors are told on their dashboard. Once the role has
moved to a mentor, or the dojo has gone dormant, the champion leaves the
list. The number next to **Privacy** in the sidebar counts them.

How long data is kept
---------------------

The site removes these by itself, every night:

- **Accounts** are deleted two years after their last login. A parent's
  account stays as long as one of their children uses their own login. The
  account holder gets a reminder 30 days and 7 days before, and an account
  is never deleted sooner than 30 days after the first reminder. A
  champion's or mentor's name stays on past sessions and on the belts and
  badges they awarded.
- **The audit log** keeps an entry as long as the account it's about, or
  that made it; an entry with no account behind it goes after two years.
- **Mail**: a year after a mail was queued, its address, subject and text are
  cleared. What kind of mail it was and whether it went out stay, for the
  figures.
- **Invitations** to the organisation are removed 30 days after they were
  accepted, withdrawn or ran out (an invitation is valid for 14 days).
- **Login sessions** end two weeks after they last changed, and are then
  removed.

Some data is removed at once, not after a period:

- **A criminal-record extract** is deleted the moment a reviewer decides on
  it. Only the decision is kept.
- **Two-step login** methods (an authenticator app, passkeys, backup codes)
  are removed when someone turns two-step login off.

For some data, how long to keep it hasn't been decided yet, so the site
doesn't remove it by itself: a child's details and their sessions, belts
and badges, past team memberships, applications, background-check
decisions, read notifications and bounce reports. These go only when the
account (or the child) they belong to is deleted.
