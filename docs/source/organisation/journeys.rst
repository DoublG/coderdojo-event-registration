Journeys
========

A journey is a campaign that runs by itself. Every day at 18:00 it sends its
mail to everyone who matches its segment that day and hasn't had it
recently. It's how you send "we miss you" the week a child stops coming, or
"welcome back" when they return.

Setting one up
--------------

Under **Journeys**, choose **New journey**. It has the same fields as a
campaign, plus **Not again for (days)**: someone who got it doesn't get it
again for that many days.

Most journeys use a segment with a rule about a change, for example "How the
child comes to sessions changed: became **At risk** in the last 7 days".

A new journey is paused. Its page shows the mail, a test button, and who
would get it on the next run. **Activate** starts it: at the next run,
everyone who matches at that moment gets it, so check that list first.
**Pause** stops it again at any time.

The results show how many people got it in the last 30 days and in total.
