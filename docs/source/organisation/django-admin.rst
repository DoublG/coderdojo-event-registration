The Django admin
================

The Django admin is the site's technical tool, for fixing data by hand when
something went wrong or has no page on the dashboard yet. It isn't open all
the time: anyone with a role in the organisation asks for it, and gets it
for 12 hours.

Asking for access
-----------------

On the organisation dashboard, choose **Django admin** (under
**Organisation** in the sidebar). Say why you need it, for example "fix a
registration by hand", enter your password and choose **Ask for access**.
You can then use **Open the Django admin** until the time shown, with your
role's permissions: an admin can change what the admin role may change, a
board member only look.

After 12 hours your access closes by itself. Done sooner? Choose **End
access now**. To continue later, ask again, with a new reason.

Who sees it
-----------

Every request is recorded, with its reason and when it ended, and the
organisation's other admins get a notification (the bell at the top of the
dashboard). They can see who has access on the People page and end it
there (see :doc:`people`). Your own earlier requests are listed below the
form.
