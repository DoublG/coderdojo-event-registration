Starting a new dojo
======================

Bringing CoderDojo to a new city or town starts with an application from
your own CoderDojo account: if you don't have one yet, create one first
(the same kind of account families use), then log in.

#. Log in and choose **Apply to start a dojo** from your account menu (or
   **Get involved** → **Start a new dojo**). Your name and email come from
   your account; add a phone number if you like.
#. Tell us the area/city the dojo would run in, your preferred schedule
   (e.g. "Saturday mornings"), and a proposed venue if you have one.
#. Add any relevant experience, a connection at the venue, or anything
   else useful for us to know.
#. Confirm you understand sessions are free and volunteer-run, and that a
   background check will be required (see :doc:`background-check`) since
   a dojo's champion works directly with minors — then submit.

What happens next
-------------------

A CoderDojo Belgium admin looks at your application and emails you a link
to upload your background check document (see :doc:`background-check`).
Once it's validated and your application is approved, you'll get an email
and a **Create a dojo** button on your account page.

Creating your dojo makes you its **champion**. It starts as a **draft**,
hidden from the public site, while you fill in its profile, add your team
and plan your first sessions; you launch it yourself when it's ready — see
:doc:`../dojo-team/running-a-session`.
