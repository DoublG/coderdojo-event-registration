Starting a new dojo
======================

Bringing CoderDojo to a new city or town starts with an application, not a
login — you don't need an account first.

.. note::

   Already have a family/guardian account? Log in first, then use **Apply
   to start a dojo** from your account menu — your name, email, and phone
   are pre-filled, and once approved the dojo-owner role is added to your
   existing login instead of a separate one being created.

#. Go to **Get involved** and choose **Start a new dojo**.
#. Fill in your name, email, and phone.
#. Tell us the area/city the dojo would run in, your preferred schedule
   (e.g. "Saturday mornings"), and a proposed venue if you have one.
#. Add any relevant experience, a connection at the venue, or anything
   else useful for us to know.
#. Confirm you understand sessions are free and volunteer-run, and that a
   background check will be required (see :doc:`background-check`) since
   a dojo's lead coach works directly with minors — then submit.

What happens next
-------------------

A CoderDojo Belgium admin reviews your application. Once your background
check clears, you'll be emailed your dojo-owner login with a temporary
password, which you'll be asked to replace the first time you log in — or,
if you applied while already logged in (see above), you'll simply be
notified that the dojo-owner role is now active on your existing account,
no new password needed. Either way, you'll be able to manage your dojo's
page and run its sessions — see :doc:`../dojo-team/running-a-session`.
