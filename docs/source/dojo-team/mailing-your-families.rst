Mailing your families
=====================

Your dojo can send its own mail to its families through the site: "no
session this Saturday", "bring a charger", or "our new sessions are open"
to the families who came this year. Only the dojo's **champion** writes and
sends it; the rest of the team can read what was sent.

Who gets it
-----------

In the sidebar, under **Manage**, open **Mail** and choose **Write a mail**.
First pick who gets it:

- **All families of the dojo**: families whose child has your dojo as home
  dojo, or came to one of its sessions in the last year.
- **Families booked for a session**: families with a place for one of your
  sessions, and if you like those on its waiting list too.
- **Families on a session's waiting list**: for example to tell them about an
  extra session.
- **Families who came recently**: families whose child came in the last 90,
  180 or 365 days.
- **New families**, **We miss you** (children who missed several sessions in
  a row or stopped coming), **Children of an age** and **Children on a
  pathway** (one of your dojo's pathways). These choose by the children's
  details, so they only reach families who agreed to that.

Under your choice you see how many families it reaches. You never see who
they are. Only families who want news from your dojo count: a family can
stop your dojo's mail, or all news from dojos, at any time.

Writing it
----------

Write a **subject** (your dojo's name goes in front of it) and a
**message**, in plain text: links work, but there's no layout, no images and
no attachments. If your dojo uses more than one language, you can write the
mail in each of them too. A family then gets it in its own language, or else
in your dojo's main language.

**Save draft** keeps it. The page then shows the mail as the families get
it. **Send me a test** sends it to your own address first.

Sending it
----------

**Send** sends it right away. The mail goes out over the next few minutes,
from CoderDojo Belgium with your dojo's name, and **replies go to your
dojo's email address**, so fill that in under **Settings** first. You can
send at most four mails in 30 days. While it's going out, **Stop sending**
stops the mails that haven't gone out yet.

Afterwards the mail's page shows how many were sent, how many couldn't be
delivered, and how many families stopped your dojo's news since.

The mail queue
--------------

**Mail queue**, on the **Mail** page, shows what happened to your dojo's
mail: your own mails and the automatic mail about new sessions. Per mail you
see how many are waiting to go out, were sent, couldn't be delivered, or
were held back, and below that why mail was held back (for example because
a family stopped your dojo's mail). It shows numbers, never addresses. Mail
that couldn't be delivered usually went to a mistyped or closed address; if
something looks wrong, ask the organisation.
