Reports for your dojo
=====================

CoderDojo Belgium prepares reports for every dojo: tables and charts with
your own dojo's figures, such as how many of your children come regularly,
or how many children of each age band come. As the dojo's **champion**, you
find them under **Reports** (in the **Insights** section) in your dojo's
sidebar. Mentors don't see them.

The reports are read-only: you see them the way CoderDojo Belgium made them,
and there's nothing to save. They only ever show your own dojo's figures,
never another dojo's. A child counts for the dojo that is their main dojo,
from last night's figures. Because your team already knows its children
from the attendance list, small groups aren't merged here: a report can
show a group of one child.

**Download the figures (CSV)** at the top right gives your dojo's figures
as a spreadsheet file.

Which reports there are, and what's in them, is decided by CoderDojo
Belgium (see the organisation's :doc:`../organisation/reports` page). If
you'd like another report for your dojo, ask them.
