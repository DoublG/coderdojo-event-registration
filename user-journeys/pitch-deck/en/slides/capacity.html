Measured on 8 October 2026 within the real hosting limits: 3 web processes and 32 database connections, fixed by the hosting package. The platform keeps at most 24 of them busy and asks everyone else to wait a moment, so under pressure it gets slower, never unreliable.

Measured on 8 October 2026 on a copy of the site set up with production's exact limits (3 web workers, at most 8 requests each, 32 database connections), plus a test through the live demo domain. 300 users, each clicking every 2 to 8 seconds: 98.1% answered, 1.9% asked to retry, 95% of pages within 96 ms. Rush: 500 families on the same five sessions within two minutes: 83.4% of requests answered, 16.6% got the "try again in a moment" page, no server errors, no session overbooked (a lock on each session decides every booking in turn). Through the live domain: 95% of pages within 110 ms. The 32 connections are part of the hosting package and can't be raised, so more capacity comes from making each page lighter.
