Who it serves

Six people, one platform

Parent

Finds a dojo, signs children up, follows their progress.

Ninja

A child with an optional login of their own.

Mentor

A vetted volunteer who helps at one or more dojos.

Champion

Runs a dojo: sessions, team, mail to families.

Reviewer

Checks criminal-record extracts and approves volunteers.

Organisation

Campaigns, content, privacy and access.
