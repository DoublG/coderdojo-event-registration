Measured on 8 October 2026 on the live hosting, with data the size we expect after a year of growth (100 dojos, 8,300 accounts) and the mail caught in a test mailbox. A longer mailing list only takes longer to send; it doesn't need more memory.

Measured on 8 October 2026: the live demo was switched to a throwaway copy of the data at the size of one year of growth (100 dojos, 8,304 accounts), and one campaign went to every adult account, launched the way the organisation dashboard does it. All 7,145 mails were queued within about 4.5 minutes, in chunks of 200, and sent in one hour at a steady 120 a minute, with none failed. The mail went to a test mailbox, never to real people. The whole platform used 1,378 MB at rest and 1,425 MB at the most; the mail worker stayed at 147 MB, under its 160 MB limit. Sending is paced on purpose (7,200 mails an hour), and booking and account mail are picked first, so a family's confirmation still arrives within seconds while a campaign is going out. A bigger audience only takes longer: memory stays the same. Details in CAPACITY.md.
