About Priya

Priya founded Ghent Dojo in 2021 after two years mentoring at a dojo in Antwerp. She's a software engineer by day and spends most Saturday mornings making sure every ninja — and every parent dropping one off for the first time — feels welcome the moment they walk in. Ask her about accessible robotics kits; it's her favourite tangent.
