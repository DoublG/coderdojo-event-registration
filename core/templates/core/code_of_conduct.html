{% translate "Code of conduct" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}A dojo is a place where children and teenagers learn to code by doing, trying and making mistakes, together with volunteers. To keep it safe and fun for everyone, everybody at a dojo follows this code: ninjas, parents and guardians, mentors, champions and visitors.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "What CoderDojo stands for" %}

  • {% translate "Free: no fees, a venue that's lent to us, and volunteers who give their time." %}
  • {% translate "Open to everyone, whatever their background, home situation, gender or disability." %}
  • {% translate "Outside school and non-commercial: nobody sells anything at a dojo." %}
  • {% translate "A warm, safe place where it's fine not to know something yet." %}

{% translate "Everyone" %}

  • {% translate "Be kind and respectful. No bullying, harassment or discrimination, at the dojo or online." %}
  • {% translate "Help each other: asking a question and sharing what you know are both part of learning." %}
  • {% translate "Take care of the venue and the equipment, and follow the dojo team's safety instructions." %}
  • {% translate "Ask before taking a photo or video of someone; for a child, ask their parent or guardian." %}

{% translate "Ninjas" %}

  • {% translate "Ask a mentor when you're stuck, and let others ask you." %}
  • {% translate "Only make and share things that are kind and appropriate, and never share your own or anyone else's personal details online." %}
  • {% translate "Look after laptops and kits you borrow, and give them back at the end." %}
  • {% translate "Tell a mentor, or your parents, if something online or at the dojo makes you feel uncomfortable." %}

{% translate "Parents and guardians" %}

  • {% translate "Children under 12 need an adult on site for the whole session; older children can be dropped off and picked up on time." %}
  • {% translate "Tell the dojo about allergies or anything else the team should know, in your child's details on this site." %}
  • {% translate "Cancel a place you can't use, so a child on the waiting list can come instead." %}

{% translate "Mentors and champions" %}

  • {% translate "Keep your background check (criminal record extract, model 2) valid; without it you can't help at a dojo." %}
  • {% translate "Stay in sight of others: don't be alone with a ninja out of view, and don't contact ninjas privately outside the dojo (messages, social media)." %}
  • {% translate "Use families' details only for the dojo, never for anything else." %}
  • {% translate "Make everyone feel welcome, first-timers most of all, and let ninjas find the answer themselves." %}

{% translate "Worried about something?" %}

  • {% translate "At a session, talk to the dojo's champion or a mentor straight away." %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed with email=organisation.email %}You can always contact CoderDojo Belgium at {{ email }}; we treat every report confidentially.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% translate "If a child is in danger, call 112." %}
  • {% translate "Worried about violence against or abuse of a child? Call 1712 (in Dutch) or Écoute-Enfants on 103 (in French), free and anonymous." %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}If someone doesn't follow this code, the dojo team can ask them to leave the session, and CoderDojo Belgium can end a volunteer's role or close an account.{% endblocktranslate %}
