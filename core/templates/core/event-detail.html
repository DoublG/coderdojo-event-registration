Scratch & Python

Sat 27 Sep · 10:00–13:15 · Ghent Public Library

About this session

A relaxed Saturday morning of small-group coding — kids pick up where they left off or start fresh, with a mentor keeping an eye on their screen the whole time. This session runs Scratch for first-timers and Python for anyone ready for a bit more. There's no fixed curriculum, so it's fine to jump between the two.

What to bring

A laptop if you have one — a small number are available to borrow on the day. Nothing else is required; mentors bring everything needed to get started.

Questions

Yes — mentors group kids by what they're working on, not experience level, so first-timers and returning coders sit side by side.

Come on in — mentors are used to kids arriving throughout the first half hour.

Under-12s need an adult on site; older kids can be dropped off.
