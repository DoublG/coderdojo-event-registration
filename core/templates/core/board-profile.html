About Aisling

Aisling joined the board in 2019 after running a dojo in Dublin, and moved to Belgium the following year. She spends most of her time making sure every dojo — from the newest in Antwerp to the longest-running in Ghent — has what it needs to keep going: mentors, laptops, a room, and a bit of paperwork sorted out so local champions can focus on Saturday mornings.
