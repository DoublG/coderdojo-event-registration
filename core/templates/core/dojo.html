About this dojo

Ghent Dojo has been running every second Saturday since 2019, hosted by Ghent Public Library. Kids work in small groups with a volunteer or Ninja mentor on Scratch, Python or web projects at their own pace — there's no fixed curriculum, so first-timers and returning coders sit in the same room. Bring a laptop if you have one; a small number are available to borrow on the day.
